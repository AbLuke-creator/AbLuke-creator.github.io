Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the private tools used to run two YouTube channels, The Line for Today and Orbit Lab: Orbit Lab uploader (one Google API project that both channels use), a small automation that reads each channel's statistics and comments each morning, and Line for Today Publisher (a Meta app for The Line for Today's Instagram account). They are used only by the owner of these channels. None is offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to them.

What they access

With the channel owner's permission, the tools use YouTube API Services on each channel's own account to: upload the channel's own videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and playlists; read the channel's own performance statistics (views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources, audience retention); read comments posted on the channel's own videos; and post replies to those comments, but only replies the owner has reviewed and approved. To compare the channels with similar ones, they also read publicly available statistics (subscriber, view and video counts, and video titles) of a short list of other public channels. They do not access anyone else's account, private data or messages.

For The Line for Today's own Instagram account, Line for Today Publisher uses Meta's Instagram Graph API to publish the channel's own videos, read that account's own post statistics, and read and reply to comments on its own posts, again with replies reviewed by the owner. It does not access direct messages or ads.

What they store, and for how long

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub account, and are sent only to Google (or Meta, for Instagram). They are never shared or published.

Channel statistics, and the comments left on the channels' own videos and posts (the comment text and the commenter's public display name), are kept in the owner's private GitHub repository so that the owner can review them and approve replies. This data is used only to run these two channels. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling, and it is not combined with data from other sources. Statistics snapshots and comments older than 30 days are deleted from this working data automatically each day, and comments leave the review queue as soon as they are answered or dismissed. Anyone who wants a comment of theirs removed from this data can delete it on YouTube or Instagram, and it will drop out at the next refresh.

Cookies and your device

The tools run only on the owner's computer and in his private GitHub automation. They do not place, read or store cookies or any other data on the devices of viewers or commenters. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

If you commented on one of these channels and want your comment removed from the working data, delete the comment on YouTube or Instagram; it drops out at the next daily refresh, and everything is removed after 30 days in any case. You can also email the address below and ask for it to be removed sooner; this is done within 7 days.

Google and YouTube

These tools use YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions (and, for Meta, by removing the app in the owner's Meta Business settings). The tools stop working until they are signed in again. Revoking access does not delete videos already published.
