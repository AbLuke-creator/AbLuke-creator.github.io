Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers two private tools: Line for Today uploader and Orbit Lab uploader. Each is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel it serves (The Line for Today, Orbit Lab) to manage that channel's own videos. Neither is offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to them.

What they access

When the channel owner signs in, the tool asks Google for permission to manage that channel's YouTube account, so it can upload videos; set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and captions; add videos to the channel's playlists; post comments on the channel's own videos; and read the channel's own performance statistics (views, watch time) to plan future videos. They do not access anyone else's account or data.

The Line for Today also posts its own videos to its own Instagram account and Facebook Page through Meta's Graph API, using the same approach: a sign-in token held by the owner.

What they store

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer and are sent only to Google (or Meta, for Instagram and Facebook). No personal data about viewers, commenters or anyone else is collected, stored, sold or shared. Channel statistics are kept only on the owner's computer, as aggregate numbers.

Google and YouTube

These tools use YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions (and, for Meta, in Facebook's Business Integrations settings). The tools stop working until they are signed in again. Revoking access does not delete videos already published.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. Questions: through the About page of either channel.
