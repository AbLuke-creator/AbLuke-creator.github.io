Two small channels, made with care.

An independent creator in Bangalore, India, running two YouTube channels.

The Line for Today

One line from a great writer every morning, and what it means for the day you're about to have. Quotes are checked against their original public-domain sources. Narration is AI-generated; the writing and design are made by a human.

Orbit Lab

Short, accurate physics and space experiments: the same thing done on different worlds, at real scale, with real numbers. Simulations, music and sound are original.

Tools

Each channel uses a small private tool, run only by the channel owner on his own computer, to upload the channel's own videos through YouTube API Services: Line for Today uploader and Orbit Lab uploader. They are not offered to the public. See the privacy policy and terms.
